Help centre

How can we help?

Find quick answers about using SmartCare and getting the most from your care workspace.

Is SmartCare a medical diagnosis service?

No. SmartCare provides educational navigation and helps you prepare for conversations with qualified professionals.

How does the AI assistant use my message?

It analyses the text you provide to identify language, symptoms, care areas, urgency signals, and relevant directory options.

What should I do in an emergency?

Call your local emergency service immediately. Do not wait for an online response.
